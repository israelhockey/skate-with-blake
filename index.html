    Questions.

    Anything not here, put it in the notes when you book and Blake will answer when he confirms.

    What is Pro-Time ice?

    Pro-Time is ice that StarCenter reserves for instructors to run private lessons. Coaches pay the rink for the session, and players book directly with their coach. That's why you request your lesson here, not at the front desk.

    How long is a lesson?

    Each lesson runs one Pro-Time session at Northlake. The exact start and end time is shown on every session you can pick.

    Do I need to be good to book?

    No. Plenty of lessons are with adult E League players in their first season. Tell Blake where you're at and the lesson starts there.

    How do I pay?

    By Zelle or Venmo, after Blake confirms your session. Nothing is charged when you send a request.

    Can I bring a friend or teammate?

    Yes. Choose semi-private for two players or small group for three to four, and add their names when you book. Groups work best when players are at a similar level.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Let Blake know as early as you can. Pro-Time ice is paid for in advance, so late cancellations may not be refundable. Blake will share his exact policy when he confirms.

    Can parents watch?

    Yes, from the stands. Most players focus better without coaching from the glass, so save the feedback for the ride home.
